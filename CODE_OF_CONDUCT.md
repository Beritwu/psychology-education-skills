# Community conduct

Treat contributors and learners with respect. Discuss evidence and ideas without
harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or disclosure of private information.
Use fictional or properly de-identified examples. Do not pressure contributors to
claim educational or clinical efficacy that has not been established.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation. To report a
conduct issue, ask a maintainer for a private channel without publishing personal
details. This project has no clinical or emergency-support function.
